title: VidMetrics Pulse
description: "Paste any YouTube channel and it flags which videos beat that channel's own baseline. Instead of search.list at 100 quota units per call, it builds the analysis from the uploads playlist for about 5-8 units, roughly 97% less. Trending is a Z-score relative to the channel, with a recency multiplier for videos under a week old, and the final score weights views, engagement, consistency and growth velocity. Share links are base64url with no database behind them."
stack:
  - Next.js
  - TypeScript
  - Tailwind CSS
  - Recharts
